## Morning Practice: Prime the Day (10-15 min)

*Integrate with your 5am routine: MOVE → REFLECT → GROW*

Choose ONE of these based on what the day holds:

### Option A: Contextual Surfacing
*Use when you have specific meetings, calls, or focused work ahead*

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
Today I'm [meeting with X / working on Y / preparing for Z].

Search /notes/ for my past thinking related to this:
- Notes about these people, projects, or themes
- Relevant frameworks or insights I've developed
- Questions I've asked before but not answered

Surface 3-5 notes I should re-read. Give me a 2-sentence summary
of each and why it's relevant to today.
```

**Why it works:** You arrive at meetings with your past thinking already activated. You don't start from zero.

---

### Option B: Pillar Activation
*Use when you want to consciously tend a specific life dimension*

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
I want to activate my thinking around [pillar name].

Search /notes/ for notes tagged with [relevant tags]:
- #ParableLabs #VentureBuilding #ClientWork (Pillar 1)
- #Wealth #FinancialStrategy #Lineage (Pillar 2)
- #Movement #Dance #AnimalFlow #Longevity (Pillar 3)
- #Permaculture #Garden #Ecological #Nature (Pillar 4)
- #Dharma #Meditation #Spirituality #Love (Pillar 5)
- #CognitiveSovereignty #KnowledgeGraph #Learning (Pillar 6)
- #Zuri #Parenting #Community #Partnership (Pillar 7)
- #InnerLife #Somatic #Healing #Aidan (Pillar 8)

Show me:
- 2-3 notes from this pillar I haven't revisited in 30+ days
- Any unresolved questions or intentions I stated
- Connections to other pillars I might explore today
```

**Why it works:** You consciously water different parts of your life garden, preventing any pillar from going dormant.

---

### Option C: Random Rediscovery
*Use when you want serendipity and creative collision*

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
Surprise me. Select 3 random notes from /notes/ that are
at least 30 days old.

For each one:
- Title and date
- Core insight in 2-3 sentences
- One question: "Given where I am now, what might I do with this?"

Prioritize notes I haven't engaged with recently.
```

**Why it works:** You rediscover forgotten insights. Old thinking collides with current context. New connections emerge.

## Pre-Creation Priming (5 min)

*Use before voice notes, writing sessions, meetings, or any generative work*

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
I'm about to [record a voice note about X / write about Y /
have a conversation about Z].

Search /notes/ for my previous thinking on this topic.
Give me:
- Key insights I've already developed
- Questions I've raised but not answered
- Frameworks or metaphors I've used
- People, books, or resources I've mentioned as relevant
```

**Why it works:** You build on your foundation instead of reinventing. Your thinking compounds.

## Evening Integration (10 min)

*Layer onto your brain-dump ritual during the 3-2-1 wind-down*

### Option A: Connection Weaving
*Use after a day with significant new input*

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
Today I [had a meeting about X / processed a voice note about Y /
learned Z / experienced W].

Search /notes/ for related past thinking:
- What connections exist to previous notes?
- Does today's experience confirm, challenge, or extend past insights?
- Is there anything I should update in existing notes?
- What deserves its own note tomorrow?
```

---

### Option B: Pillar Check-In
*Use for intentional life review*

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
Do a quick scan across my 8 pillars based on recent notes:

1. Which pillars have I been actively feeding this week?
2. Which pillars are quiet or neglected?
3. Are there any cross-pillar connections I'm missing?
4. What's one small action I could take tomorrow for
   an under-tended pillar?
```

---

### Option C: Dialogue with Past Self
*Use for reflection and accountability*

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
Find a note from 60-90 days ago that contains a decision,
prediction, or intention I stated.

Show me what I said then, and ask me:
- Did this play out as I expected?
- What do I know now that I didn't know then?
- Would I make the same choice today?
- What has this taught me about myself?
```

**Why it works:** You create a feedback loop between intention and reality. This is how you learn from your own thinking.

## Weekly Deep Dive (30 min)

*Integrate with Sunday planning session*

### Synthesis + Pattern Recognition

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
Review all notes modified in the last 7 days.

Create a weekly synthesis that includes:
1. **Themes**: What topics dominated my thinking?
2. **Pillar balance**: Which pillars got attention, which didn't?
3. **Emerging patterns**: What's trying to emerge that I should name?
4. **Action items**: What commitments did I make? What's unfinished?
5. **Connections**: What unexpected links appeared between notes?
6. **Questions**: What am I sitting with that needs more exploration?

Save this as a synthesis note: YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-synthesis.md
```

---

### Thematic Deep Dive

**Cowork Prompt:**
```
I want to deep dive into #[tag or theme].

1. List all notes with this tag, sorted by date
2. Summarize the arc: How has my thinking evolved?
3. Identify the 2-3 most insight-dense notes
4. Surface contradictions or tensions across notes
5. What's the next question I should explore here?
```

## The Flywheel Effect

When this practice is working:

```
Morning surfacing → activates relevant past thinking
        ↓
Pre-creation priming → ensures you build on foundations
        ↓
Daily work → generates voice notes, meetings, transcripts
        ↓
Processing → turns raw input into enhanced notes
        ↓
Evening integration → weaves new into existing
        ↓
Weekly synthesis → reveals patterns across days
        ↓
Monthly connection discovery → surfaces hidden threads
        ↓
[Cycle repeats with compounding value]
```

Each cycle deposits more value. Each retrieval strengthens connections. The graph becomes a cognitive exoskeleton—it remembers what you forget and surfaces what you need.

## Start Simple

Don't try to do everything. Start with:

1. **One morning prompt** during REFLECT block (5:20-5:40)
2. **One evening prompt** during brain-dump
3. **Weekly synthesis** during Sunday planning

Once these feel natural, add special practices as contexts arise.

The goal isn't perfect consistency—it's making the knowledge graph part of how you think, not just where you store.
